## A little about me

I'm a developer interested in the space between useful software and ambitious ideas.

My projects explore different ways technology can be built, experienced, and improved — from AI assistants that interact with people and their workflows to tools for understanding security in connected systems.

I like working across different layers of a project: the underlying logic, the architecture, the interface, and the details that make the final experience feel complete.

I don't want every project to look or behave the same. Each one starts with a different question and develops its own direction.

**Areas I enjoy exploring**

- Artificial intelligence and personal assistants
- Software architecture and automation
- Cybersecurity and distributed systems
- Developer tools and desktop applications
- Interface design and product experimentation
